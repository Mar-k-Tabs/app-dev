# Godzilla Minus One & Minus Zero

## About the Movies

**Godzilla Minus One** (2023) is my *favorite movie so far*. It was directed by **Takashi Yamazaki**, who also wrote it and supervised the visual effects.

> "War reduced Japan to zero, and Godzilla plunged it into minus."

---

## Why I Love It

1. The **story** is emotional and human, not just monsters.
2. The **visual effects** look amazing, even on a small budget.
3. Godzilla feels truly *terrifying* again.

### Things That Stand Out

- Post-war Japan setting
  - A country already rebuilding from nothing
  - Ordinary people fighting back
- Powerful music and sound design
- Strong themes of survival and guilt

## Fun Fact

The title comes from the idea that Japan started at `zero` after the war, and then Godzilla pushed it to `minus one`.

```text
Godzilla Minus One -> Godzilla Minus Zero
```

Learn more on the [official Godzilla site](https://www.godzilla.com).
